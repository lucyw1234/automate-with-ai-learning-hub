THE PHILOSOPHY

Tools will change.
The thinking won't.

A new agent can launch tomorrow. That should expand your toolbox, not reset your learning. The durable skill is knowing what the business needs and choosing the simplest system that can solve it reliably.

BUSINESS PROBLEM→ARCHITECTURE→RIGHT TOOLS→BUSINESS OUTCOME
CHOOSE THE APPROACH

Workflow, agent or both?

Don't start with a product name. Start with the nature of the work. Use this guide before you build.

Simple rule: predictable steps → workflow. Contextual judgment → agent. Most serious business systems → a deliberate combination of both.

THE AI BUILDER'S TOOLBOX

Choose by capability, not loyalty.

DON'T DEFAULT TO ONE TOOL

CURATED FREE RESOURCES

Official docs first. Tutorials second.

Use documentation to build independence. Videos are useful for explanation, but avoid becoming dependent on exact-copy tutorials.

DON'T LEARN THIS YET

You don't need to master the entire AI internet.

Don't disappear for months trying to learn Python, JavaScript, Docker, every vector database, every framework, MCP and multi-agent orchestration before you can solve one useful business problem.

Focus: Problem → Architecture → Workflows & APIs → LLMs → Agents → Knowledge → Production
